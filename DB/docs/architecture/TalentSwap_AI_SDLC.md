## 1. Governance Rules

This document is now the **permanent development process** for TalentSwap.

Nobody, including the Lead Architect, may change this process unless one of the following conditions is met:

1. The change is **explicitly approved**.
2. A **critical architectural issue** is discovered.
3. A formal **Architecture Decision Record (ADR)** is created.

Otherwise, the process remains **unchanged and locked**.
